# SharePoint Enterprise Hub – Information Architecture (IA)

## 1. Purpose
The Enterprise Hub provides a unified digital workplace for:
- Organization-wide communication
- Department collaboration
- Document management
- Process automation
- Search & knowledge management

## 2. Site Hierarchy
Hub Site
 ├── HR Portal
 ├── Finance Portal
 ├── IT Support Portal
 ├── Projects & PMO Portal
 ├── Policies & Compliance Center
 └── Knowledge Base (KB)

## 3. Navigation Model
### Global Nav
- Home
- Departments
- Projects
- Knowledge Base
- Policies
- Helpdesk

### Local Nav (Example: IT Portal)
- Announcements
- Ticketing
- SOPs
- Asset Management
- FAQ

## 4. Content Grouping
- News & Communications
- Documents (policies, forms)
- Lists (assets, incidents, tickets)
- Pages & web parts
- Workflows (automation)

## 5. Security Model (High Level)
- Hub Visitors: read-only
- Hub Members: contribute to specific lists/pages
- Hub Owners: full control
- Department Owners: manage own portal
- External Users: controlled via sharing policy
